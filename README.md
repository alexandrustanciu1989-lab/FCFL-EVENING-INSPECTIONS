# ForeverClean Routes — Company Presentation

Slide deck for presenting **ForeverClean Routes** (demo: [cardinal-routes.vercel.app](https://cardinal-routes.vercel.app/)) as a faster alternative to ServiceCore for cardboard & recycling fleets.

## Open the deck

Open `index.html` in a browser (or serve the folder).

- **Desktop:** arrow keys, space, click Prev/Next
- **iPad:** swipe left/right, or tap Prev/Next (44px targets)
- **Jump:** `#3` in the URL for slide 3
- **Presenting:** Safari fullscreen / Add to Home Screen works best landscape

On iPad and tablets the head-to-head slide switches from a wide table to side-by-side comparison cards so text stays readable.

## What’s in the pitch

1. Title — built for the route, not the spinner  
2. Why we’re here — same purpose as ServiceCore, less friction  
3. Speed race — every tap compounds across 70+ stops  
4. Driver-named ServiceCore pain points  
5. Head-to-head comparison table  
6. Driver app advantages  
7. Live dispatch board advantages  
8. Offline / sync for NC dead zones  
9. 15 feedback items already answered in the demo  
10. Business case for ForeverClean  
11. 5-minute live demo plan  
12. The ask — pilot on real routes
